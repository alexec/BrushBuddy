# Brushwise Support

Brushwise walks you through a complete oral-hygiene routine: water flosser, floss,
a two-minute brush (plus tongue), and mouthwash, with a weekly toothbrush check.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't a stage have a timer?**
Water flossing and flossing take as long as they take. Tap **Next** when you are done.
Brushing and mouthwash are timed to the durations dentists recommend.

**Can I skip a stage?**
Yes. In **Settings → Your routine**, switch any stage off for the morning or the
night. Brushwise respects your choice but keeps recommending the full routine.

**Where are my reminders?**
Check **Settings → Reminders** in Brushwise, then make sure notifications are allowed
for Brushwise in the iOS Settings app.

**How does Apple Health logging work?**
Turn on **Log to Apple Health** in Settings and allow Brushwise to write Tooth
Brushing data. Each completed brushing appears in Health with its real start and end
time. Brushwise never reads your Health data.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Everything stays on your device. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
