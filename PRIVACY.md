# Brushwise Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

Brushwise is a guided tooth-brushing routine app for iPhone. It is designed so that
your information stays on your device.

## What Brushwise stores

- **Routine history and settings.** Which stages you completed, when, and your
  preferences (reminder times, sounds, haptics, toothbrush replacement date). This is
  stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.

Brushwise has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. The app does
not use the network at all.

## What Brushwise does not do

- It does **not** collect analytics or usage statistics.
- It does **not** use advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
- It does **not** share any data with the developer or with anyone else.

## Apple Health

If you turn on **Log to Apple Health** in Settings, Brushwise asks iOS for permission
to **write** Tooth Brushing events to the Health app. Each completed brushing is saved
with its start and end time. Brushwise never reads data from Health, and Health data
is never sent anywhere. You can revoke this permission at any time in the Health app
(Profile → Apps → Brushwise), and you can delete the records there.

Health data written by Brushwise is not used for advertising or marketing and is not
disclosed to third parties, in line with Apple's HealthKit requirements.

## Notifications

Brushwise can send twice-daily local reminders. These are scheduled on your device
and do not involve any server. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings.

## Your data, your control

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of its data.
Health records you chose to log remain in the Health app until you remove them there.

## Children

Brushwise does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
